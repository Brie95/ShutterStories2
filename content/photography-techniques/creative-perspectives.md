
# Creative Perspectives

## Seeing Differently

Perspective transforms ordinary scenes into extraordinary images. Changing your angle, distance, or lens choice can reveal new stories and emotions. Creative perspectives help you stand out and develop your personal style.

### Ways to Change Perspective

- **Low Angle:** Makes subjects appear powerful or dramatic.
    
- **High Angle:** Creates vulnerability or context.
    
- **Close-Up:** Highlights texture and detail.
    
- **Wide Angle:** Emphasizes environment and scale.
    

### Techniques to Try

1. Shoot through objects like leaves or windows.
    
2. Experiment with reflections in water or glass.
    
3. Use foreground elements to add depth.
    
4. Tilt your camera for dynamic diagonal lines.
