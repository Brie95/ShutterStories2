Portrait Techniques

## Capturing Personality

Portrait photography is about more than faces — it’s about emotion, connection, and storytelling. A great portrait reveals something meaningful about the subject, whether subtle or bold.

### Essential Portrait Tips

- **Eye Contact:** Sharp eyes create instant connection.
    
- **Angles:** Slight head turns add dimension and reduce flatness.
    
- **Backgrounds:** Choose simple backgrounds to keep focus on the subject.
    
- **Posing:** Guide your subject gently; natural poses feel more authentic.
    

### Working With Natural Light

Portraits thrive in soft lighting. Golden hour adds warmth, while overcast skies create flattering shadows. Backlighting can produce dreamy hair glow.
