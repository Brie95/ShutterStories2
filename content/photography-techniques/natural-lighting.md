
# Natural Lighting

## Understanding Light

Light is the most important element in photography. Natural light changes throughout the day, creating different moods and textures. Learning to work with sunlight helps you capture images that feel authentic and dynamic.

### Types of Natural Light

- **Golden Hour:** Soft, warm, flattering light shortly after sunrise or before sunset.  
    
- **Blue Hour:** Cool, atmospheric light just before sunrise or after sunset.  
    
- **Midday Sun:** Harsh and bright; best for dramatic shadows or high‑contrast scenes.  
    
- **Overcast:** Diffused light ideal for portraits and even exposure.  
    

### Tips for Better Lighting

1. Position your subject facing the light for clarity.
2. Use backlighting for silhouettes and glow.
3. Look for natural reflectors like walls or sidewalks.
4. Avoid direct overhead light unless you want strong shadows.
