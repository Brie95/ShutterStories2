
# Action Photography

## Capturing Movement

Action photography freezes moments that happen too fast for the human eye. Whether it’s sports, wildlife, or street scenes, mastering movement requires timing, anticipation, and technical control.

### Key Settings

- **Fast Shutter Speed:** 1/500 or faster for crisp motion.
    
- **Continuous Autofocus:** Tracks moving subjects.
    
- **Burst Mode:** Captures multiple frames per second.
    
- **Wide Aperture:** Helps isolate subjects and maintain brightness.
    

### Techniques

1. Pre‑focus on the area where action will occur.
    
2. Pan your camera to follow motion for artistic blur.
    
3. Shoot from low angles to add drama.
    
4. Use leading lines to emphasize direction.
