
# Composition Basics

## Why Composition Matters

Composition is the foundation of strong photography. It determines how viewers experience your image and where their eyes travel first. Good composition helps you tell a story, guide attention, and create emotional impact. Even simple subjects become powerful when arranged intentionally.

### Key Principles

- **Rule of Thirds:** Divide your frame into a 3×3 grid and place subjects along the lines.  
    
- **Leading Lines:** Use roads, fences, shadows, or architecture to guide the viewer’s eye.  
    
- **Framing:** Surround your subject with natural frames like windows or branches.  
    
- **Balance:** Distribute visual weight so the image feels stable.
